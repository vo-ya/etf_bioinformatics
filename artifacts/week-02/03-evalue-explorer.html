Increasing effective database length tenfold increases E tenfold. Adding one bit halves E at fixed search space. E is a count that can exceed 1, not the probability that a particular hit is false. There is no universal threshold that proves homology or function.
Optional equation and assumptions

E = m × n × 2−S′. Here m and n are synthetic effective lengths in amino-acid residues. Inputs are varied independently to isolate the relationship; bit scores are not the raw +2/−1/−2 DNA scores from the other demonstrations.

Real BLAST search statistics depend on the scoring model, effective-length calculations, composition and other settings. This calculator does not calculate those corrections or search a database.
