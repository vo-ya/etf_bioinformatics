What the two edge models mean
Unique mode merges identical k-mer types and records how often they were observed. The baseline has six observations but five unique edges: GAC was observed twice. Traversing the five unique edges once happens to reconstruct our restricted error-free toy ATGACCT. That rule is not a general assembler.
Occurrence mode treats every observation as a separate directed edge. With baseline overlapping reads this duplicates GAC; the resulting graph need not admit a single edge-covering walk. This does not mean the genome contains two consecutive GAC copies. Real assembly must address coverage, repeat multiplicity, errors, missing sequence and other information.
The repeat preset has six unique 3-mer edges. Two complete edge orders beginning at AT spell ATGATCAT and ATCATGAT. The graph cycle does not prove the molecule is circular. A longer read spanning the branch can distinguish the alternatives. Choosing a route alone supplies no new observations.
