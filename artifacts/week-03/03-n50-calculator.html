Calculating N50 and L50

Sort contigs from longest to shortest. Add their lengths until the sum reaches half the assembly length. N50 is the length of the contig at that threshold.

What N50 measures

N50 is the length of the contig that first makes cumulative length reach at least 50% of the assembly's own total length, after sorting longest first. L50 is the number of contigs needed to reach that point. The denominator is not an assumed true genome length.

An incorrect join can increase N50 by turning two contigs into one longer contig. To judge the assembly, inspect the sequences and read support for the joins, and check for missing or contaminating sequence. Empty assemblies have undefined N50 and L50; this calculator asks for at least one positive whole-number length.
