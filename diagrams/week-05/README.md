# Week 5 original figures

Run `python3 diagrams/week-05/build_diagrams.py` to regenerate the six SVGs.
All examples and compositions were drawn for this course. Figures are shared
between the presentation and student guide; no upstream images were copied.

- `pileup.svg`: six A and six G at 1-based position 101.
- `sequence-changes.svg`: deletion, duplication, and insertion of oriented blocks.
- `inversion.svg`: reversal of both block order and orientation.
- `translocation.svg`: a balanced reciprocal terminal exchange between toy chromosomes.
- `coverage.svg`: equal 100-base windows; region [400,800) mean 10, flanks 20.
- `deletion-evidence.svg`: a 300-base sample fragment spans 700 reference bases
  across a 400-base deletion; a separate split read joins [380,400) and [800,820).

Block diagrams are schematic; the coordinate diagrams have explicit scales.
The pileup and coverage examples are separate datasets. Biological definitions
and representation conventions are linked in the student guide and slide notes.
