Worked answers and questions to preserve

  • BAM record: SAM is the readable text representation; BAM is compressed binary. Coordinate sorting and an index support region queries, not variant inference. POS=99 and CIGAR=5M place TCGAT across positions 99–103, giving G at 101. D in QUAL encodes Q35; MAPQ 60 describes placement. M permits mismatches.
  • Site B: raw 6/14 G; after base Q≥20 and mapping Q≥30, 0/8. Changing the denominator is essential. This removes observed alternate support under that rule; it does not prove absence.
  • Sites A and C: both 6/12 G, but A has 3/3 forward/reverse G and C has 6/0. Ask for independent reverse support and alignment context. Do not present strand imbalance as an automatic error verdict.
  • Site D: all six observations have base Q20 and mapping Q30. Inclusive default thresholds retain them. Q≥30 removes them all. An undefined fraction is None, not zero.
  • Likelihood: six A and six G give log₁₀L −12.026, −3.612, −12.026 for 0/0, 0/1, 1/1 in the binary e=0.01 model. No observations make all likelihoods equal. Counts alone cannot distinguish A from C.
  • Indel ambiguity: C-AAG, CA-AG, and CAA-G all spell CAAG. Distinguish an equivalent representation from a resolved biological history.
  • Depth: [400,800) has values 10,9,11,10, mean 10; eight flanks have mean 20. Ratio=0.5. The duplication example has mean 30 and ratio=1.5. These copy-number interpretations assume comparable sampling and mapping.
  • Junction: paired alignments [250,300) and [900,950) span 700 reference bases; removing 400 leaves a 300-base sample fragment. A separate 40-base split read joins [380,400) to [800,820). The depth bins alone do not establish exact breakpoints.
  • VCF: toy:101 maps to Python index 100. POS=501 REF=AT ALT=A removes T at 502 (Python index 501), with A as the anchor. Supplied GT and PASS values were not inferred by our simple filters.

If students equate high read count with confidence, ask how many original molecules contributed. If they equate ordinary depth with no SV, return to the balanced inversion. If they equate PASS with biological effect, ask which filter or experiment addresses that effect.

Closing examples: mutation consequences

Slides 39–40 use the standard genetic code and show mRNA 5′→3′, with U rather than DNA T. GAA→GAG is synonymous (Glu→Glu); GAA→GUA is missense (Glu→Val); GAA→UAA is nonsense (Glu→STOP). Ask why an unchanged amino acid does not exclude an effect on splicing or RNA stability. A premature stop can lead to transcript degradation or a shortened protein.

For the indel, keep the AUG start fixed. Removing the first G after AUG from AUG GAA CCG UUU UAA gives AUG AAC CGU UUU AA…: Met–Asn–Arg–Phe…, with an incomplete final codon in the displayed fragment. Removing GAA instead gives AUG CCG UUU UAA: Met–Pro–Phe–STOP. Ask which deletion preserves the downstream grouping. A net coding insertion or deletion divisible by three preserves the frame, but can still affect function. Briefly distinguish stop-loss, altered splicing, and regulatory changes; consequence depends on the transcript and does not establish pathogenicity.

Homework support and optional work

The student notebook uses complete cells and explicit editing tasks rather than unfinished function stubs. Students submit their notebook with predictions, changed outputs, and explanations. The instructor edition supplies written answers without changing the computational examples. The likelihood extension is optional and adds 10–15 minutes beyond required work.

Keep the two types of coordinates visible: VCF POS is 1-based; Python intervals use zero-based, excluded stop boundaries. The VCF parser handles only the two literal biallelic examples with one sample. It deliberately does not support structural breakend syntax, all FORMAT combinations, or a general VCF workflow.

Additional BAM notebook

The separate BAM exercise and instructor answers extend slides 3–4 into real Python file operations. Allow 75–90 minutes separately; specify whether students should do it as additional practice or as a replacement for part of another assignment. The existing class and original homework allocations are unchanged.

Prepare pysam 0.24.1 before class if package access is unreliable. The notebook generates its own reference, unsorted BAM, coordinate-sorted BAM, and index in a fresh temporary directory. It has 28 records but 27 query names because the paired fragment has two records. At position 101, the raw pileup has A=6/G=15; the explicit quality and flag policy retains A=6/G=6. The written subset has 15 records, including three with low base quality, because record selection and base selection are different operations. At reference indices 149–150, a deletion alignment overlaps but contributes no A/C/G/T base.

Emphasize zero-based numeric pysam intervals versus one-based SAM text POS, reverse-aligned SEQ orientation, and the difference between an alignment record and an independent molecule. The flags and mappings are invented. The supplied filters illustrate a simple SNV policy and are not a general SV or clinical workflow.
